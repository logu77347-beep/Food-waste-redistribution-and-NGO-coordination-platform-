Five modules, one pipeline

Each step of a donation maps to one module in the backend design.

Module 1

Accounts & roles

Registration, token-based login and role checks for donors, NGOs and volunteers. NGO KYC checks plug in here.

Module 2

Donation listing

Food type, quantity, coordinates and shelf life. Anything past its safety window is marked expired.

Module 3

Geo matching

Haversine distance picks NGOs within 10 km and sorts the nearest first.

Module 4

Reservation lock

The first NGO to claim wins. Everyone else sees that it is already taken.

Module 5

Dispatch & proof

A volunteer picks up, delivers, and closes the donation with a verification PIN.

Production tech stack

The backend in this repo is Flask, SQLite and PyJWT. Swap SQLite for PostgreSQL + PostGIS when you scale.
